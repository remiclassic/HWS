Questions

Which phones will it run on?

iPhone and Android. The same app, built from one codebase.

What happens to my email?

It goes on the launch list and nowhere else. You'll get one email when Spotter is available, and possibly an invitation to test it early. It isn't shared or sold.

Where does the car data come from?

From official assortment sheets and packaging where they exist, and from collector communities where they don't. Every field in the app shows which, with a confidence score, so you can judge it yourself.

Is this an official Hot Wheels app?

No. Spotter is an independent tool made by collectors. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Mattel.
